# Urban 2040: Flow Pods & Gemini Intersection

An intelligent, sustainable urban transit and living infrastructure conceptualized for the year 2040. This project replaces traditional congestion-heavy transit systems with modular **Flow Pods** coordinated in real-time by a Google Gemini neural intelligence layer, seamlessly embedded within vertical green-facade architecture.

## 🏙️ Project Overview
Urban 2040 tackles modern congestion, carbon emissions, and pedestrian isolation by decentralizing public transit and infusing streetscapes with generative AI orchestration. By leveraging Google Gemini as an adaptive urban layer, traffic flow dynamically shifts based on citizen density, weather, and real-time transit demands.

---

## ⚙️ Key Architecture & Features
* **AI-Coordinated Flow Pods:** Modular, autonomous transit units that dock on-demand, bypassing fixed rigid timetables and traditional bus congestion.
* **Gemini Adaptive Street Grid:** Neural light overlays projected onto roadways that guide autonomous traffic and pedestrian flow safely in real-time.
* **Living Vertical Facades:** Building exteriors integrated with hydroponic trellises and smart agricultural sensors to improve air quality and urban heat-island mitigation.
* **Dynamic Curbside Docking:** Multi-tiered passenger stations with automated boarding zones and real-time capacity monitoring.

## 🔄 System Flow
1. **Demand Prediction:** Gemini ingests real-time neighborhood foot-traffic and mobility requests.
2. **Dynamic Routing:** Pods are dispatched and routed along illuminated dynamic lanes on the street grid.
3. **Boarding & Docking:** Passengers interface with automated curbside stations with real-time status updates (*"Docking in Progress"*).
4. **Ecosystem Balancing:** Building facades regulate ambient temperature and clean exhaust emissions synchronously with transit operations.
